## What’s new

- **Send feedback.** Found a bug or have an idea? Choose Help → Send Feedback…, or Settings → General → Feedback, pick what kind it is and write a few lines.
- **Only what you write.** Feedback sends your message and, if you add one, an email address for a reply. With “Include app version and device” on, it also sends the app and macOS version, the kind of Mac and your language; “What gets sent” shows exactly that. Your notes are never included.
- **Works offline too.** If the feedback can’t be delivered right away, it’s kept and sent shortly. If sending fails, Email Instead opens a message to feedback@matilde.page.
