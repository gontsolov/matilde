## What’s new

- **See which AI tools can reach your notes.** If you’ve connected an AI tool such as Claude or ChatGPT to Matilde, Settings → Sync now lists it: its name, what it’s allowed to do, when the connection ends and when it was last used.
- **Disconnect in one click.** Choose Disconnect… next to a tool and it stops at once. To use it again, connect it from the tool.
- **Comments say who left them.** A comment an AI tool adds to your note shows “From” and the tool’s name.
- **Clear about encryption.** While an AI tool is connected, Settings → Sync says so: the Matilde server can then decrypt your notes for that tool. Without one, your notes stay end-to-end encrypted and no one else can read them.
- **Updates through matilde.page.** Matilde now checks for updates at matilde.page. Each check is counted anonymously by app and macOS version, and nothing else is sent.
