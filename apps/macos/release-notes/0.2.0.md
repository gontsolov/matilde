## What’s new

- **Sync (beta).** Matilde can now keep your notes in sync between your Macs, end-to-end encrypted: only your Macs can read your writing. Sync is off until you turn it on in Settings → Sync. Sign in with your email address and the code sent to your email.
- The first time you turn sync on, Matilde shows your **recovery key** once. Keep it somewhere safe: it is the only way to unlock your notes on another Mac, and Matilde can’t recover it for you.
- If the same note was changed on two Macs at once, nothing is lost: the other version is kept as a branch named after the other Mac, such as “Title — from MacBook Air”.
- A note you move to the Trash on one Mac goes to the macOS Trash on your other Macs, so you can still get it back.

## Before you turn on sync

Sync is new and still in beta. Keep a backup of your writing folder before you turn it on.

## Known limitations

- If both Macs already have a note with the same name, the second one appears as “Name 2” for now.
- There is no way yet to print or replace your recovery key.
