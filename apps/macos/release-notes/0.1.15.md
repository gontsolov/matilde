## What’s new

- Comments are always visible in the right margin, beside the passages they belong to. Ask Matilde conversations and your private comments share one column, in passage order.
- The text and the comment column are centered together with even margins, so the page feels balanced. The title, goal and draft controls line up with the body, and headings are a little lighter.
- Ask Matilde passages get a subtle underline with a faint tint, stronger while you hover or open the conversation. Private comments keep their yellow highlight.
- Cleaner comment cards: the reply box sits alone at the bottom, **Accept** sits inside the suggested rewrite, and an archive button appears at the top right on hover. Archived conversations stay in Review history.
- Stash now opens from the tray button in the toolbar (or Shift-Command-J).
- Branching an alternative counts up (“Alternative 2”) instead of repeating the suffix.
- Fixed a crash when typing with spelling correction on. Spelling underlines now sit just below the words.
