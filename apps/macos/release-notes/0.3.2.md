## What’s new

- **Feedback, one click away.** There’s a small Feedback button at the bottom of the sidebar, opposite Settings.
- **A simpler feedback form.** It opens in its own small window, centred on your screen, instead of hanging off the Settings window. It’s more compact, with a smaller title, and the extra switch is gone: the app version and the kind of Mac are always included, and your notes never are.
- **Clearer privacy wording.** The app and matilde.page now say it plainly: your notes are encrypted on your devices, and no one else can read them.
