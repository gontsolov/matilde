## What’s new

- **A friendlier way to turn on sync.** Settings → Sync now walks you through setup one step at a time, with the same steps and words as the iPhone app we’re building: what sync does, your email, then the code we send you.
- **A proper code input.** The emailed code goes into six separate boxes. Type it, paste it with ⌘V (spaces and dashes are fine) or let macOS fill it in, and it’s sent as soon as it’s complete. Backspace and the arrow keys move between boxes, a wrong code gives a little shake, and you can ask for a new code after 30 seconds or change your email.
- **Your recovery key, easier to keep.** A new account shows its recovery key in clear groups with Copy, Save… and Print…, and you confirm you’ve kept it before moving on. Where iCloud Keychain is available, Matilde also keeps a copy there, so the next device can fill in the key for you.
- **Settings → Sync at a glance.** Once sync is on, you see its status, the account you signed in with and the devices signed in to it, with Sync Now and Sign Out.

## Good to know

Sync is still in beta. Keep a backup of your writing folder before you turn it on.
