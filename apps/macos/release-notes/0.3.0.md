## What’s new

- **Your Matilde key: 12 words.** The key that unlocks your synced notes on a new device is now 12 everyday words instead of a long code. It’s shown on a card with Copy, Save… and Print…, and you tick “I’ve saved my Matilde key” before moving on.
- **Easier to type in.** Entering your key gives you 12 word fields with suggestions as you type, flags a word that isn’t right, and fills every field at once when you paste the whole key. A built-in check catches most typos and swapped words before anything is sent.
- **Your old key still works.** If your account was made with an earlier version, choose “I have a key that starts with MTLD” and enter it as before. Making a new key later switches your account to words.

## Good to know

- **Update your other devices.** Accounts made with this version use the 12-word key, which earlier versions (0.2.x) can’t read. Update every Mac to 0.3.0 before signing it in to a new account.
- Sync is still in beta. Keep a backup of your writing folder before you turn it on.
