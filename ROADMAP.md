# Matilde roadmap

Our living backlog for features, ideas, fixes, and open questions. Save new ideas here; update entries as we build. Product direction: a quiet native writing experience, tactile drafts on a canvas, and AI as a supporting feature.

Last updated: 2026-11-18.

## How we use this

- “Save this idea”, “later”, and “document as a follow-up” mean capture it here, not start building it.
- Give each item a stable ID, a status, and a concrete outcome. Keep the user's intent and link to detailed design notes when useful.
- Statuses: **Idea** (uncommitted), **Planned** (agreed direction), **In progress**, **Blocked** (state the dependency), **Done**, or **Dropped** (keep the reason).
- Before building, review this backlog and update the relevant item. Record newly discovered fixes and questions without presenting unverified concerns as confirmed bugs.
- After building, record what works and how it was checked. Mark Done only when the stated scope is complete; keep unfinished parts as separate entries.
- Priority/order is provisional until we choose the next task. Saving or completing an item does not authorize a release.

| SYNC-01 | In progress | **Live database out of the workspace.** Urgent: `.matilde/workspace.sqlite` sat inside the workspace (default `~/Documents/Matilde`), where iCloud Drive “Desktop & Documents” or Dropbox can corrupt a live SQLite file. One-time migration on opening a workspace: SQLite online backup into a temporary file beside the target (folds in a `-wal`, rolls back a hot journal), integrity check, row counts per table compared, atomic rename into place, then the old file (and any sidecars) renamed to `workspace.sqlite.migrated`, never deleted. Another Mac sharing a synced workspace that migrated first: the next Mac imports the newest `workspace.sqlite.migrated` and leaves it in place. No old database: a fresh one. Failed copy: the old database stays live in place for that session and nothing is renamed; retried at next launch. A moved workspace keeps its store; a copied one (Finder duplicate) gets its own ID and a copy of the original's data. Review data stays in SQLite, never in Markdown. Tests: `WorkspaceStoreTests` (fresh, migration and idempotence, uncheckpointed WAL, interrupted copy, crash after copy, earlier `.migrated` kept, second Mac, missing and unreadable old file, moved and copied workspace, malformed ID).
