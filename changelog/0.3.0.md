The key that unlocks your synced notes is now twelve everyday words, much easier to write down and to type in.

- Your Matilde key appears on a card with Copy, Save and Print, and you confirm you've kept it before moving on.
- When you type it in, each word has its own field with suggestions, and a built-in check catches most typos.
- An older key that starts with MTLD still works.
- Update all your Macs to this version before signing them in to a new account.
