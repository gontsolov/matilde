Matilde's very first versions: a quiet place to write, where any draft can branch into another.

- Write in plain Markdown files, in a folder on your Mac. Formatting appears as you type, without the symbols getting in the way.
- **Branch a draft** to try another version. The page curls over, and both drafts stay yours to edit.
- Pinch out to see every version of a note laid out on a board, then pinch back into the one you want.
- Paste images and links straight into your writing. Images stay on your Mac.
- A welcome note shows you around. Reopen it any time from the Help menu.
- Bold, italics, lists and checkboxes behave the way your fingers expect.
