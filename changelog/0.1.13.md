matilde: You write, I read. That's the deal.

Matilde learns to read along. Ask for a review and get a few thoughtful notes beside your writing, never a rewrite you didn't ask for.

- **Writing review.** Connect your Langdock account in Settings and ask for a review. A few comments appear beside the passages they're about. A suggested rewrite only goes in if you accept it, and Undo takes it back.
- **Ask about a passage.** Select some text and ask a question. The conversation stays with that passage, and each draft keeps its own.
- **Compare side by side.** Open two drafts next to each other, both editable, with the changed lines gently highlighted.
- Type / to add a heading, a list, a checkbox, a quote or a divider from a small menu.
- Only the draft you're working on is read, and only when you ask or when you've turned on automatic reviews.
- Matilde now runs on Apple silicon Macs. Intel Macs can keep using version 0.1.8.
