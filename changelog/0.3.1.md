Found a bug, or have an idea? You can now send it from inside Matilde.

- Choose Help → Send Feedback…, pick what kind it is and write a few lines.
- Only your message is sent, plus an email address if you'd like a reply. Your notes are never included.
- Offline? Your feedback waits and goes out shortly after.
