Sending feedback got simpler, and the privacy wording got plainer.

- A small Feedback button now sits at the bottom of the sidebar.
- The feedback form opens in its own small window and asks for less. It always includes the app version and the kind of Mac, and never your notes.
- The app and matilde.page now say it plainly: your notes are encrypted on your devices, and no one else can read them.
