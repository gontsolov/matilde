The writing assistant has a name now. Ask Matilde reads along, asks questions, and keeps her notes exactly where they belong.

- Press Shift-Command-A to ask about what you've selected, or about the paragraph you're in. On an empty line, she reviews the whole draft.
- Comments stay on their passages while you write. They move with your edits and only go away when you delete the passage or the comment. Undo brings them back.
- **Comments of your own.** Add a private note to any passage with Shift-Command-K. It gets a soft highlighter mark and never changes your text.
- Tell Ask Matilde how you'd like her to read, in Settings → Ask Matilde.
- Drag notes between folders, start a new note right inside a folder, and search from the top of the sidebar.
- No more scrollbars, for a calmer page.
