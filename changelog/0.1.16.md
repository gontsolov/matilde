Comments move into the margin, right beside the words they're about, like an editor's notes on a printed page.

- Ask Matilde's comments and your own share one column next to your text, in the order they appear.
- The text and the margin are centred together, so the page feels balanced.
- Passages Matilde commented on get a quiet underline. Yours keep their yellow highlight.
- Archive a conversation when you're done with it. It stays in your review history.
- New, lighter icons throughout the app.
- Branching counts up ("Alternative 2") instead of piling on suffixes.
- Fixed a crash when typing with spelling correction turned on.
