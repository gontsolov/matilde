A tidier top of the window, and the things you do often are now a right-click away.

- Right-click a note, a folder or empty space in the sidebar to create, branch, duplicate, rename or move notes, or to show them in Finder.
- The sidebar button stays in the same place whether the sidebar is open or closed.
- A roomier window header, with search sitting neatly below the window buttons.
