Every update now comes with a short, friendly note about what changed for your writing.

- After an update, Matilde shows you what's new once, in a small window of its own. Look again any time under Help → What's New in Matilde…
- The update window now tells you what a new version brings in plain words before you install it.
- Every past release is collected at [matilde.page/changelog](https://matilde.page/changelog).
