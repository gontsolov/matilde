Settings of your own, a stash for the bits that don't fit yet, and a dark mode for late nights.

- **The Stash.** A scratch pad beside your draft for cut sentences, loose ideas and links. Every version of a note shares it, and Shift-Command-J opens it.
- **Settings.** Choose your text size and line spacing, and turn spelling underlines on or off.
- **Light or dark.** Follow your Mac, or pick one yourself.
- The sidebar shows when each note was last edited, with the most recent first.
- Move between the title, the goal and your text with the arrow keys or Tab.
- A refreshed app icon, with a little raised tilde.
