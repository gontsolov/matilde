matilde: Six little boxes for your code. Very satisfying.

Setting up sync is friendlier now, with one calm step at a time.

- Settings → Sync walks you through it: what sync does, your email, then the code that arrives by email.
- Type or paste the code into its six boxes. It's sent as soon as it's complete.
- Your recovery key is shown in easy groups, with Copy, Save and Print. Where iCloud Keychain is on, Matilde keeps a copy there for your next device.
- Once sync is on, you see its status, your account and your devices at a glance.
