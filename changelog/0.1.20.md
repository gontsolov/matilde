Your comments and conversations move somewhere safer, and Ask Matilde reads the whole draft for better answers.

- Comments and conversations are now kept on your Mac, outside your writing folder, so iCloud Drive or Dropbox can't damage them while syncing. They move by themselves the first time you open this version. Your notes aren't touched.
- When you ask about a passage, Ask Matilde reads the rest of your draft too, so her answers fit what you're writing.
- Once you've updated, don't go back to 0.1.19: it won't find your comments in their new place.
