title: Your notes on every Mac
matilde: Start a sentence on one Mac, finish it on the other.
---

Sync arrives, in beta. Your notes travel between your Macs, encrypted on your devices, so no one else can read them.

- Turn it on in Settings → Sync and sign in with your email address and a code. It stays off until you do.
- The first time, Matilde shows you a recovery key. Keep it somewhere safe: it's the only way to unlock your notes on another Mac.
- If a note changed on two Macs at once, nothing is lost. The other version becomes a branch named after that Mac.
- A note you move to the Trash on one Mac goes to the Trash on the others, so you can still get it back.
- Sync is new, so keep a backup of your writing folder before you turn it on.
