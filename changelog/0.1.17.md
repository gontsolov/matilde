The window loses its top bar, and everything that isn't your writing steps back while you type.

- The controls float over the page and fade away as you write.
- Notes open at the top. Control-Command-Up and Down move to the previous or next note, and an empty note invites you to start writing.
- Calmer conversations: your messages in bubbles, roomier cards, and a small dot on threads with something new.
- Pick an app icon in Settings → General: Matilde, Leather, Neon, Blueprint or Chalk.
- Headings, paragraphs, lists and quotes follow one type scale, so the page reads more evenly.
- New branches keep the comments from the draft they came from.
