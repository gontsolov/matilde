title: Everything a keystroke away
matilde: Press ⌘K and tell me where to go.
---

Getting around your notes and shaping your writing now takes fewer clicks and fewer surprises.

- Press **⌘K** to jump to any note, run any action or open a Settings section. Type a few letters, in any order, and Matilde finds it.
- Lists behave the way your fingers expect: Tab and Shift-Tab nest and un-nest items, Return on an empty item ends the list, and numbers keep themselves in order.
- Backspace at the start of a heading, list item, checkbox or quote removes just its marker, and an emptied heading no longer leaves a stray # behind.
- Each of these edits undoes in one step, and the arrow keys glide past hidden formatting.
- Ask Matilde now reads the comments already on your draft, so a review no longer repeats points you've already discussed.
- Drag notes in the sidebar to put them in your own order or move them into another folder.
- When a note has no goal, the sidebar shows how it begins, and the space above the title is calmer.
