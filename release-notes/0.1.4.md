## Settings, your way

Open **Matilde → Settings…** or press **Command-comma**.

- Adjust writing text size and line spacing, with a live preview.
- Turn spelling underlines on or off, or restore the writing defaults.
- Show or hide the dotted canvas.
- Save, replace, or remove a Langdock API key in macOS Keychain. Saved keys are never displayed or stored in your writing files.

This prepares Matilde for future AI features. No writing is sent to Langdock, and no AI requests are enabled yet.
