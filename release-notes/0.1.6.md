## New: Stash

Keep loose thoughts, cut passages, and reference links in a Markdown stash shared across a draft family. Open it from the bottom-right corner or with Shift-Command-J; Escape tucks it away.

- Independent autosave, undo, and remembered writing position.
- A taller paper panel that meets the bottom edge, with aligned text and a quick spring animation.
- Shared notes survive branching, renaming, and removing drafts. The Writing menu provides Finder access to retained notes and recovery files.
- A hands-on stash exercise in new welcome documents; existing writing is preserved.

## Writing and navigation

- Roomier line spacing in the editor and stash, with aligned wrapped lists.
- Stash grows vertically with its contents and uses a compact pull tab.
- Sidebar documents sort by recent edits and reopen the last-used draft, with a compact branch count.
- Move between title, goal, and body with arrow keys, Enter, or Tab. Typing a divider places the caret on a new line.
- Better caret and inline-code alignment.
- Copy Markdown from the top-right document menu.

## Appearance

Choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings, now accessible from the bottom-left of the sidebar. Dark mode uses a restrained, neutral palette.
