## What's new

- Pinch out of the editor to follow the page continuously onto the draft board, with easing and rounded corners. Short or reversed gestures return to writing.
- Zoom into the page under your pointer, with the board anchored to that point.
- Press Command-Delete with the sidebar focused to move the selected file to Trash. In the editor and search, the shortcut retains its normal text-editing behavior.
- Document titles, goals, and draft controls scroll with the page; a compact toolbar title appears when the header leaves view.
- Press Return in the writing goal to save and focus the editor. Shift-Return remains available for line breaks.

Physical trackpad feel and end-to-end gesture delivery still need broader hands-on verification.
