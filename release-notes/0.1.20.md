## What’s new

- Comments, Ask Matilde threads and unread state now live in `~/Library/Application Support/Matilde` instead of inside your writing folder, where iCloud Drive or Dropbox syncing could damage them. They move there automatically the first time 0.1.20 opens your workspace; the old file stays behind as `.matilde/workspace.sqlite.migrated`. Your notes are not touched.
- Ask Matilde now reads your whole draft with the passage you asked about marked, so its answers fit the rest of your writing.

## Before you update

After updating, don’t go back to 0.1.19: it won’t find your comments and threads in their new place.
