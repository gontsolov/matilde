## What’s new

- Your own comments now appear in the margin too. Click a card to reveal its passage, and new branches keep the comments from the draft they came from.
- Switching notes opens them at the top. Control-Command-Up and Control-Command-Down move to the previous or next note, and an empty note shows a “Start writing…” placeholder.
- Calmer comment threads: a centred reply box with a soft fade behind it, your messages in bubbles, wider cards, and a thread that stays where you scrolled while a reply streams in. Shift-Return adds a new line.
- Choose an alternative app icon in Settings → General: Matilde, Leather, Neon, Blueprint or Chalk.
- The window no longer has a top bar. The controls float over the page and fade away while you type.
- Threads with something new show a small dot, and margin cards slide in and out.
- The slash menu is lighter and more compact, and opens above the line you are writing on.
- Headings, paragraphs, lists, quotes and code follow one type and spacing scale, so the page reads more evenly.
- The whole page scrolls, including margin cards that sit below the end of the text.
- A rewrite suggested in a reply now appears in the thread where it was offered, instead of above the conversation.
