## What’s new

- Writing assist is now **Ask Matilde**. Review, pause/resume and review history live in one toolbar control, so the side pane shows only comments and conversations.
- Press Shift-Command-A to ask about the selection, or about the current paragraph when nothing is selected. On an empty line it reviews the draft.
- Comments now stay on their passages as you write. They move with edits before them, grow or shrink with edits inside them, and disappear only when you delete the whole passage or the comment itself. Undo brings them back. Title and goal changes no longer clear them.
- Delete a conversation with the new trash button.
- Edit the guidance that shapes Ask Matilde’s reviews and replies in Settings → Ask Matilde, with Reset to Default.
- Add private comments with Shift-Command-K or the context menu. Commented passages get a highlighter mark; click it to edit or delete the note. Comments stay in the workspace and never change your Markdown.
- Drag notes between folders in the sidebar, create a note inside a folder with its `+` button, and use the always-visible sidebar search.
- Scrollbars are hidden throughout the app.

Suggested rewrites still require explicit acceptance, and can only be accepted while their passage is unchanged.
